OUR PLACE IN THE ’VERSE

A guild without the second job.

We like coordinated play, reliable crewmates and having an actual plan. We also remember that Star Citizen is where we come to relax, improvise and occasionally discover that somebody forgot to close the ramp.

The organisation sits inside the wider Minervan Vanguard community, so members can stay connected even when the current patch, ship or game is not cooperating.
