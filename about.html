A new banner for an old crew.

Minervan Vanguard was founded in 2024 around a core of players whose friendships stretch back as far as eighteen years.

Join the communityRead our story
2024

Where it started

From Renaissance Kingdoms to many worlds.

Long before Minervan Vanguard had a name, members of its core were already playing together in Renaissance Kingdoms. Across games, platforms and years, those friendships held.

The group later spent time beneath the Athenian Legion banner. When it was time to build something of our own, Minerva captured the qualities we valued: judgement, strategy and a distinctly Roman character. Vanguard reflected where we preferred to be when a fight began—at the front.

What we are like

Mature. Relaxed. Organised only when it helps.

01

Welcoming by default

Anyone can join. We are always looking for fun, friendly people rather than demanding a particular résumé of games or ranks.

02

Global, with busy time zones

Members are spread around the world, with the largest groups across the UK and Europe, and North America.

03

Casual, not careless

We are relaxed and rarely serious for long, but we can organise properly when a war, operation or project calls for it.

18years of shared history at the longest end

Why it lasts

The games change. The humour does not.

Shared humour, long memories and real-world meetups have kept the core connected well beyond any single MMO. The average game may not survive eighteen years. The group already has.
