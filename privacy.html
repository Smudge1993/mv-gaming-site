Plain English privacy

What this website records—and what it does not.

Minervan Vanguard is a community website. We do not operate user accounts, process payments or sell visitor information.

LAST UPDATED 2 AUGUST 2026
AT A GLANCENo advertising profiles

No analytics cookies are set by this site. Contact information is only received when you choose to email us.

Information collected automatically

Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand broad website use and performance, including page views, referral information, browser and device characteristics, approximate geographic information and Core Web Vitals. The Cloudflare analytics beacon is designed without client-side analytics cookies or localStorage and is not used by us to build advertising profiles.

Google Search Console

Google Search Console provides aggregated information about how this site appears in Google Search, such as search queries, impressions, clicks, position and indexing status. Search Console does not add a separate visitor-tracking script to this website.

Information you provide

If you contact help@mvgaming.org, we receive the information included in your message and the email address used to send it. We use it only to respond, deal with the issue raised and maintain an appropriate record of the conversation.

Third-party websites

The site links to services including Discord, Roberts Space Industries, Star Citizen and Torn. Their own privacy terms apply after you leave mvgaming.org.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not use analytics cookies. Some linked third-party services may use cookies or other storage when you visit them. The Star Citizen page may remember your soundtrack mute preference in your browser.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Website measurement data is processed by the providers named above under their own terms. Emails are retained only for as long as reasonably needed to answer or manage the matter raised.

Your questions

For privacy questions, corrections or deletion requests relating to information you sent directly to us, email help@mvgaming.org.
